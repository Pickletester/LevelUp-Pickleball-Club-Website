🟡 SUMMER OF PICKLEBALL

Program Dates: June 15 – July 27

LevelUp Pickleball Club's 7-Week Youth Clinic Program is built for kids who want to learn the game, level up their skills, and have a blast doing it. Whether your child is brand new to a paddle or already eyeing junior tournaments, we have a program that fits.

All clinics are led by our experienced LevelUp pros on our world-class indoor courts — air-conditioned, professional, and right here in Canonsburg, PA.
