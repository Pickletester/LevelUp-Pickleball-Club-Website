A RARE ADVERTISING OPPORTUNITY

The LevelUp Pickleball Club opened in the fall of 2025. This 60,000-square-foot sports center is the finest indoor pickleball facility in the country. Amenities include a bar and restaurant, locker rooms, a darts area, office space, a golf simulator, a speakeasy, a 3,200-square-foot banquet room, and 16 courts that are interchangeable for pickleball, table tennis, and badminton.

Hundreds of participants of all ages play daily. Seniors fill the courts every morning, kids take over in the afternoon, and workers fill them again each evening. There are over 650,000 residents living within an 8-mile radius of the facility, and we project over 100,000 annual visitors through memberships, drop-in play, leagues, events, and tournaments.

This presents a tremendous opportunity for any business looking to connect with an enthusiastic, engaged, local audience — day after day, all year long.
