Please refer to the LevelUp Pickleball Club membership page for details regarding the cost of each membership, initiation fees and costs of the various sessions for each of the membership levels.

📝 Member Registration Session

Each new member will sit down with a LevelUp Pickleball Club representative to compile your membership profile. At that time, you will also be instructed on how to download and use the LevelUp Pickleball Club app.

📱 The LevelUp Pickleball Club App

The LevelUp Pickleball Club app is used to schedule all play at the club. Download it free from the App Store or Google Play — just search "LevelUp Pickleball Club." Once it's installed, sign in or create your account and you're ready to start booking.

The app also manages all of your financial transactions. When setting up the app, you will be able to enter your (secure) payment information in the form of a credit card or bank checking account. Once a payment system has been established, your monthly membership fee and any other expenditures will automatically be deducted.

The app can also send out alerts — please keep an eye on the "Alerts" icon at the bottom of the screen. For your convenience, LPC announcements can be viewed on the app's "Home" page.

Check-in Procedure

As an LPC member, you will have the opportunity to register in advance in the LevelUp Pickleball Club app for a pickleball play session. Diamond Members can register 2 weeks in advance, Silver Members one week in advance, and Student Members 2 days in advance. All members are advised to register for play in advance.

All players must stop at the front desk prior to entering the facility. If you have pre-registered for a session, you can scan the bar code on your phone (in the LevelUp Pickleball Club app). Front Desk personnel will then advise you to enter or finalize your payment if a payment is due for the session. (You can also pre-pay for an event in the app.)

If you are new and unfamiliar with the play at LPC, let the Front Desk know and they will arrange for one of our Ambassadors to meet you and take you into the facility.

🎓 New Player Programs

Each new LPC member who is a beginner is able to take advantage of the New Player Program, which consists of the following:

Intro to Pickleball

One of the LPC pros will conduct a 30-minute introduction to pickleball to introduce you to — or expand your knowledge of — the rules of the game. This is scheduled twice per week/month as needed. After the 30-minute session, new players will have an opportunity to meet and play with some experienced members.

Beginner Clinics

Beginner clinics run by our LPC pros will be available to develop basic pickleball skills. Please refer to our weekly schedule on the LPC website and the LevelUp Pickleball Club app for times and cost.

Initial Visits

As a new player, you may not know other members and may be unfamiliar with the open play during drop-in sessions. If so, please notify the Front Desk personnel when checking in so we can get one of our Ambassadors to introduce you to others and to play some pickleball.

📊 Club Ratings & Court Assignments

LevelUp Pickleball Club uses a "Club Rating" to establish a skill level for our members, used in designated skill-level sessions. There are five specific club rating levels at LPC: 2.0–2.99, 3.0–3.49, 3.5–3.99, 4.0–4.49 and 4.5 and above. We believe that the most enjoyable pickleball matches for LPC members are those played with others who possess similar skill levels.

When becoming a member, if you have an established DUPR rating, you can use this rating to obtain a Club Rating. If a new member does not have a DUPR rating, the Front Desk will have an LPC Ambassador observe you and either assign you to a 2.0–2.9 or a 3.0–3.4 skill level. If you want to qualify for a 3.5 or higher rating, you will need to sign up and participate in a Club Rating session. These are held regularly and are listed on the LevelUp Pickleball Club app. New members who want to test for a 3.5 rating level or higher may play at that level for up to 30 days prior to testing. After 30 days, if a new member has not tested, they will receive a club skill level rating of 3.0–3.4.

Once a Club Rating is assigned, registration for LPC sessions will be restricted according to skill level. Players must only play on those courts that are assigned to their designated skill level.

FIND YOUR GAME

Whatever your style or skill level, there's a session with your name on it — every single day of the week.

🟡 Pickleball Play at LPC

Our club has several types of sessions that should fit whatever style of play you like. The LevelUp Pickleball Club app lists the various sessions by date.

Ladders

Players are initially listed according to skill level — highest to lowest. Groups of 4 and 5 players of similar skill levels play a round robin format. The player with the best score moves up the ladder and the player with the lowest score moves down. A fun and competitive format.

Round Robins

Players are randomly chosen as partners and opponents for timed games. This gives players the opportunity to play with and against a variety of players in the session. You may play six or seven games during the session. A fun format.

Up/Down Splits

You will play six or seven games on several courts. Courts are labeled highest to lowest. If you and your partner win, you both move to the next-highest court, but then play against each other. Losing teams move down a court and also play against each other. This format is fun and competitive — having a friend one game and a foe the next!

Social Events

These events have a variety of formats: round robins, up/down splits, up the river–down the river, and more. Most importantly, they are to have fun and meet other players. Time is always left at the end of these sessions to stop in the Baseline Bar & Grill to socialize and get re-energized!

Open Play (Drop-in)

These sessions are listed on the schedule as "Drop-in" sessions. The courts are separated by the five different skill levels, shown on the large TV monitor on each court. There is a centralized paddle rack for each skill level. Paddles are stacked on the racks in groups of 4 to identify the next 4 players to go onto the next available court for that skill level. Groups play only one game and then exit the court.

During Drop-in sessions there are two types of play: Qualified Play and Flex-Skill Play. Some sessions are always designated as Flex-Skill Play (afternoons and those sessions during the same time as programmed play, i.e. ladders). Other sessions are designated as Qualified Play where skill level is strictly enforced. However, the last hour of the Qualified Play sessions reverts to the Flex-Skill format to allow all players to intermix.

Qualified Play

Members must have an assigned club rating, and non-members must have a qualifying DUPR rating, to play on the associated skill-level courts. Players can drop down one skill level to play on a court of a lower skill level; however, there can be only one higher-level skill player in a group of 4.

Flex-Skill Play

All players have the ability to queue their paddles and play on a court skill level of their choosing. However, it is strongly recommended that players only move up one skill level to play on that court. All players can move down to a lower level if desired.

✨ Club Etiquette & Culture

LevelUp etiquette and culture emphasizes respect, safety, and efficiency in an active indoor setting. Key points include:

  • Please be kind, polite, respectful, and inclusive to all members and guests.
  • Maintain a positive, welcoming environment for players of all skill levels.
  • Follow the posted instructions for the Paddle/Stacking Player Rotation System during Open Play sessions.
  • Play on courts designated for your skill level as posted on the LED TV court monitors.
  • Play one game on a court and then exit the court.
  • Demonstrate good sportsmanship, win or lose. Make fair ball calls and consider a redo if your opponents object to your call.
  • If your ball goes on another court and is not going to interfere with their play, please wait until their point is done before retrieving it.
  • Please remove your empty bottles and other trash from occupied areas.
  • Keep all personal belongings (water bottles, clothing, gear) out of the court areas.
  • Keep the game fair, friendly, and most importantly — have FUN!

Not Permitted

  • No swearing, arguing, or intimidating threats.
  • No verbal threats or offensive gestures.
  • First offense is a verbal or email warning. Second offense is removal as a member for one year.

Dress Code

  • All guests must wear appropriate sports attire while on court.
  • Tops/shirts are required (tank tops are not allowed for men, and sports bras must be covered by tops/shirts for women).
  • Athletic shorts or pants.
  • No offensive or inappropriate images or words on clothing.
  • Tennis/pickleball-specific shoes (running shoes are not advised).

Baseline Bar & Grill

Darts

The Baseline Bar & Grill has 3 professional dart boards. Order your food and drinks and get in a few friendly games.
