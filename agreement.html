Membership Agreement

LevelUp Pickleball Club — Membership Agreement · 12-Month Term
Effective October 1, 2026 · 33 Mayview Road, Canonsburg, PA 15317

1. Membership Term & Dues

Membership is a 12-month commitment billed monthly. Diamond is $129/month, Silver is $29/month, and Junior (21 & under) is $29/month. This Agreement does not automatically renew; continued membership after the term requires your affirmative renewal acceptance.

2. Initiation Fee

There is no initiation fee for existing active members.

3. Court Reservations

Court rates are per player, per hour. Peak hours are 7–11 a.m. and 5–11 p.m.; off-peak is 11 a.m.–5 p.m. For reservations of 1–3 players, the minimum is based on the reserving member. The total charge is the greater of the combined per-player charges or the applicable minimum. All players must be listed, the reserving member must play, and fewer players attending does not reduce the charge. Rates and times are subject to change.

4. Cancellations & No-Shows

Court reservations must be canceled by 9:00 p.m. the prior evening; late cancellations and no-shows remain responsible for the applicable charges and minimums. Drop-ins must be canceled at least 3 hours before the session; a late-cancel or no-show Diamond Member is charged $10, and all other players their applicable drop-in fee.

5. Other Member Rates

Wednesday Ladies Up/Down Split: $10. Friday/Saturday Pickleball Socials: $10. Open tournaments: event rates apply. Lockers: Diamond $10/month; Silver/Junior $15/month.

6. Term, Payment & Early Cancellation

You authorize recurring monthly dues to your payment method on file. Except for statutory cancellation rights, early cancellation requires payment of 50% of the remaining dues and at least 30 days’ written notice to info@leveluppickleballclub.com. Downgrades may take effect after the current term.

7. Diamond Medical Pause

Diamond Members may elect a $65/month pause for up to 3 consecutive months, once per year, for surgery with a doctor’s note. Benefits and pricing are suspended while paused, and the term extends by the number of paused months. This option does not limit rights provided by law.

8. Pennsylvania Health Club Act — Your Consumer Rights

This Agreement is intended to be subject to the Pennsylvania Health Club Act. Among other rights, you may cancel without penalty within three (3) business days after signing and receiving a completed copy; you may have cancellation or extension rights for qualifying club closures, disability, death or relocation; and you must receive any refund required by law within the statutory time period. Statutory rights control over any inconsistent term. If the Club ceases operations and does not offer a comparable alternate location within ten miles, no further payments are due under this Agreement.

9. Acknowledgment

By signing, you confirm you have selected the membership indicated; understand the dues, fees, booking/cancellation rules and 12-month commitment; agree to follow Club rules and safety policies; authorize recurring monthly charges to your payment method on file; and acknowledge receipt of a completed copy of this Agreement. Optional program and event fees are charged only when used or registered for.
